# SHATTER Reel / TikTok Script (45-60 sec)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, talking head, raw single-take feel, handheld. Captions burned in (80% watch muted). Cut on every sentence. Voice: warm, steady and sisterly, like telling your best friend the truth over coffee; soft where it's tender, firm where it's truth. Bold white text with orange highlight on key words. Background: dawn light or a plain wall, no music bed for the hook (silence stops the scroll), add a low, slow drum/pad from the Biblical Truth beat on.

**Keyword:** `SHATTER`

---

## 1. THE HOOK (0:00-0:04)
**Visual:** Close on face, no intro, no "hey guys." Hold a chain link or a plain hand-gesture of breaking something. Caption: `THE CYCLE ISN'T A "PHASE."`

**Say:**
> "Sis, you're not stuck because you're weak. You're stuck because nobody told you this chain was never yours to carry."

*(Alt hooks to A/B test:)*
- "If you keep going back to the same thing and hating yourself for it, this one's for you, love."
- "Nobody at church says this out loud, so I will, girl: your cycle has a root, and Jesus already cut it."

## 2. THE "ME TOO" VIBE (0:04-0:16)
**Visual:** Quick cuts: hood up at night, phone glow on a face, empty fridge, hands shaking over a steering wheel, a cracked mirror. Or you on camera, lower voice.

**Say:**
> "I've been there, girl. Telling myself 'this is the last time' while I was already lying to myself. Smiling in the pew on Sunday, numb by Tuesday. Making promises at 2 a.m. I couldn't keep by 2 p.m.
> Same bottle. Same phone. Same guy. Same people who don't want to see me healed.
> Maybe you're doing it right now, scrolling, because it's easier than being honest.
> Me too, sis. And you are not the only one."

## 3. THE BIBLICAL TRUTH (0:16-0:34)
**Visual:** Bible in hand, or text overlay of the verse. Slow push-in. Caption pops word-by-word.

**Say:**
> "Psalm 107:14. 'He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and brake their bands in sunder.'
> Look at who's doing the work. HE brought them out. HE broke the bands.
> You've been trying to snap a chain with your own hands, and that's why you're so tired.
> And John 8:36: 'If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free INDEED.'
> Not free-ish. Not free until Friday. Free indeed.
> That chain is not your identity, beautiful. It's a lie you agreed with. And Jesus doesn't negotiate with lies."

## 4. THE ACTIONABLE TOOL (0:34-0:46)
**Visual:** Flat-lay of the printed A5 kit tucked into a Bible; flip through Day 1 to Day 7 pages, show the checklist, prayer panel, sunrise/anchor/camo cover.

**Say:**
> "So I made you something. The 7-Day Shattering Chains A5 Bible Insert Kit.
> One page a day. A real verse. A no-fluff breakdown. A checklist you actually tick off. A private page for the things you can't say out loud. And a prayer in plain, real-girl language, no churchy filler.
> Print it. Fold it. Put it in your Bible. Seven days."

## 5. THE OUTRO / INVITATION (0:46-0:54)
**Visual:** Back to face, close, steady. Point at camera.

**Say:**
> "Don't watch this and go back to the same night, love. Make one move. Right now. Not tomorrow. Today is the day that chain gets loose."

## 6. THE AUTOMATION TRIGGER (0:54-0:60)
**Visual:** Big on-screen text `COMMENT "SHATTER"` with an arrow pointing to the comment button. Hold 3 seconds.

**Say:**
> "Comment the word SHATTER. It lands in your inbox instantly. Free. Go, girl."
