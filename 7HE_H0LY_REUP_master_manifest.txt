PART I: THE BREAKING PHASE (Days 1-5)
------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1: The Bathroom Floor
  [SCRIPTURE] Psalm 34:18. "The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and
    saves the crushed in spirit."
  [APPLICATION] Hebrew root shabar = to break. God is near to the broken.
  [SYMBOL] Cracked tile with wild roses coming through.
  Soft action: 5-breath grounding. Optional line: "I am still here,
    and God is near."
Day 2: The Counterfeit Re-Up
  [SCRIPTURE] Isaiah 55:2 (spend money on what is not bread; listen,
    and eat what is good).
  [APPLICATION] Name the triggers, plan a substitute, call one person.
    Add: relapse is not disposal. Treatment and support belong here.
  [SYMBOL] Hourglass turning debris into stars.
Day 3: The Trauma Bond   (was "Narcissistic Grip")
  [SCRIPTURE] Galatians 5:1 (ESV): "For freedom Christ has set us free;
    stand firm therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery."
  [APPLICATION] Safety planning, trusted support, and a no-contact or
    low-contact decision made at the reader's own pace. Leaving is not
    easy. Forgiveness does not mean restored access.
  [SYMBOL] Padlocks becoming doves.
Day 4: The Mind Audit
  [SCRIPTURE] John 10:10 (thief comes to steal, kill, destroy; Jesus
    came that they may have life abundantly).
  [APPLICATION] Notice the thought, test it against what is true, tell
    someone. Not every thought is demonic; some are trauma echoes.
    Replaces "outside injection" script.
  [SYMBOL] Microphone, barbed wire, light.
Day 5: The Vineyard Realignment
  [SCRIPTURE] John 15:2 (the Father prunes branches that bear fruit so
    they bear more). Greek kathairo = to cleanse/prune.
  [APPLICATION] Pruning is Jesus' work on a vine already in Him. It does
    not mean abuse, abandonment, or loss were good or sent by God.
    Dead-weight audit is OPTIONAL and grief-first.
  [SYMBOL] Vine on a wooden cross.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART II: THE BUILDING PHASE (Days 6-10)
------------------------------------------------------------
Day 6: The Courtroom
  [SCRIPTURE] Psalm 43:1 ("Vindicate me, O God, and defend my cause").
  [APPLICATION] God as advocate is a picture, not a legal strategy.
  [SYMBOL] Gavel into dove and scales.
Day 7: The Witness
  [SCRIPTURE] Revelation 12:11. Greek martyria = witness/testimony.
  [APPLICATION] Share only what serves the listener and what you are
    ready to share. Privacy is allowed.
  [SYMBOL] Woman on a storm peak with a shield.
Day 8: Image Bearer
  [SCRIPTURE] Genesis 1:27 ("So God created man in his own image").
  [APPLICATION] Hebrew tselem = image, like a statue representing a
    king. Dignity comes from God, not from your past or your performance.
    (Removed "exact shadow snapshot" wording; not supported.)
  [SYMBOL] Cracked mirror, sunrise.
Day 9: The Safe Place
  [SCRIPTURE] Psalm 91:1-2 ("shelter of the Most High... my refuge and
    my fortress"). The "military outpost" claim is dropped; v2 supports
    refuge/fortress.
  [APPLICATION] Nervous-system rest, safe housing, safe people.
    Do not claim Psalm 91 guarantees no harm.
  [SYMBOL] Open fortress gate, overflowing well.
Day 10: Write It Plain
  [SCRIPTURE] Habakkuk 2:2 ("Write the vision; make it plain on
    tablets, so he may run who reads it").
  [APPLICATION] One written next step per week, not a guarantee of
    outcome.
  [SYMBOL] Blueprint scroll becoming vineyard rows.
