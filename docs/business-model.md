## The model in one sentence

A free, generous guide that meets tired people in their own language, gives them one small step at a time, and keeps pointing past Nikki, past the brand, to the OG: God Almighty. The money comes from low-priced tools that help people take those steps. Nobody is pushed, pressured, or promised anything.

## Why it's different

Most faith-and-business brands sell the founder. This one is built to **make itself less necessary**. Every product, post and chatbot answer ends the same way: a step under ten minutes, a way to check the source yourself, and an invitation to ask God directly.

- **Language we already speak.** Street words for loyalty and survival, flipped holy. "OG" means *original, the one who was there first and stayed.*
- **A constant reminder.** Every touchpoint carries the signature close: *"We survived. Now let's go back, with truth and a hand out. Souls are attached to my obedience. God Almighty gets the glory."*
- **The invitation.** Look at a real survivor's story. Test it. Ask the hard questions. Ten minutes of setting the weight down costs nothing. *What is there to lose?*

## The Three Rounds (Ethiopian coffee ceremony as the structure)

The Ethiopian coffee ceremony is poured in three rounds: *abol, tona, baraka*. [verify with a source from the community you're citing] Guests stay for all three, and the third is the blessing. The model borrows that shape: hospitality first, depth later, blessing last. Nobody is rushed out and nobody is made to stay.

| Round | What it is | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Abol (the welcome)** | This chatbot, free printables (focus checklist, 30 Minutes with God journal), short posts | Free | Be seen, be safe, get one step |
| **2. Tona (the table)** | Complete devotionals and workbooks, low-cost digital products | Low. Always with a free low-energy alternative | Walk a 7 to 30 day stretch with someone beside you |
| **3. Baraka (the blessing)** | Community, live Q&A, deeper study of the Ethiopian canon, peer encouragement (not therapy) | Optional, fair, sliding where possible | Pay it forward: a hand out to the next person |

A share of Round 3 funds Round 1 for people who can't pay. **[Nikki decides the %]**

## Foundation: Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo, not Western

- Canon, doctrine and liturgical tradition come from the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and reliable scholarship on it.
- Every Scripture quote cites **edition, translator, book, chapter, verse.** Western translations appear only to document omissions or differences.
- Dead Sea Scrolls are historical evidence, labeled separately from canon and doctrine.
- Church teaching, Scripture, history, scholarly theory, testimony and metaphor are always labeled apart.

## The content engine (what and when)

One weekly rhythm one woman can keep:

| Day | Post type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Teaching with a source |
| 2 | Practical step (under 10 minutes) |
| 3 | Testimony (only what's safe to share) |
| 4 | Question to the audience |
| 5 | Scripture or history |
| Weekend | Rest. Rest is part of the model. |

Every post follows the same bones: **hook on a real struggle, direct teaching with evidence, a step under ten minutes, a verification invitation, the standard invitation.** There's no magic posting time. Use each platform's free insights for four weeks and let your own numbers decide.

## Where each skill fits

- **GitHub** keeps every printable, devotional and the chatbot in one place you own. **GitHub Pages** publishes the chatbot free.
- **SEO** lets hurting people find you when they search ("how to start over after abuse," "what is the Ethiopian Orthodox canon"). Slow, honest, months not days.
- **Understanding people** means seen, then safe, then trusted. Give exits, skip shame, answer the three silent questions: *Is this real? Do they get it? Will they judge me?*

## What we never do

- Promise income, healing or results
- Fake urgency, countdowns or FOMO
- Say struggle means weak faith, or that praying away replaces recovery work
- Use tarot, divination, or generational-curse explanations for medical or trauma conditions
- Attack whole groups, cultures or denominations (critique specific quotes only, with sources)
- Present Nikki as healed all the way or spiritually superior
- Share anyone else's pain without permission
