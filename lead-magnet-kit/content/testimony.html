Where This Came From

This was not built on a clean stage. It was built on the floor.

I lost my sister to addiction. I have survived homelessness, trafficking, abuse, addiction, and more than a decade of manipulation in a destructive relationship. There came a point when I was alone, terrified of people, and struggling to trust anybody.

When I reached for help, too often I found strings attached. I met people who could talk recovery and quote Scripture and still see somebody's wounds as a business opportunity. I needed somewhere that could hold the truth about trauma without pushing God out of the room. I wish my sister had somewhere like that to reach. I can't say it would have changed her outcome. I can build toward making that kind of support more available to somebody who is still here.

My American Bully, Big Boy, stood beside me through some of my loneliest days. God met me while I was still trying to get up. I know what it is to go looking for another plug. In my own testimony I call God the OG Plug, and that is my metaphor, my way of pointing old language toward the Source of my hope. It is not a title from Scripture.

I am not finished, and I am not a professional. I am building as I go, and reaching back with what I have learned, what Scripture actually teaches, and something practical you can use.

— Nikki “Hellshaker” Heller
