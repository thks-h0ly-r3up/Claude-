My Testimony

I know what the floor feels like. Overdose broke me. And I have learned that D.O.A. is God's favorite starting point — because when there was nothing left of me to work with, He still had plenty to work with.

I lost my sister, Brandi Renee. I wish she had somewhere to reach. I couldn't go back and build that place for her, so I'm building it now for whoever is sitting in a car tonight wondering where to go.

I'm not a doctor and I'm not perfect. I'm a woman who is still choosing the next right thing, with Jesus as my foundation and a 90-pound gray-and-white American Bully named Big Boy who stayed beside me while I was learning how to stay beside myself.

If you are on the floor right now: baby, I know what far gone looks like. Now let me show you what God can do from there.

— Nikki “Hellshaker” Heller
