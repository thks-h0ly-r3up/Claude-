Day 1 of 7

Tell the Truth

Psalm 34:18 (ESV)“The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit.”

You can't fix what you won't name. Today isn't about fixing. It's about saying out loud where you actually are — not where you told everybody you were.

Understanding why you got here is not the same as excusing it. Both are true: I understand why I became this way, and I'm still responsible for what I do next.

Tool: The Truth Inventory

• What am I using, doing, or hiding right now?

• Who is it costing besides me?

• What am I most afraid will happen if I stop?

Do This Today
Write the three answers above — honestly
Say one true sentence out loud to God
Tell one safe person: “I'm struggling.”

Prayer

Lord, I'm done pretending. This is where I really am. I'm not asking You to be impressed — I'm asking You to meet me here. Amen.

Day 2 of 7

Get Safe

Psalm 46:1 (ESV)“God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”

Before anything else: are you safe tonight? From a substance, from a person, from yourself? Safety comes before strategy.

If someone is hurting or controlling you, leaving is not simple and nobody gets to shame you for how long it takes. You never owe anyone access to you because you forgave them.

Tool: My Safety Plan

• 3 people I can call (any hour)

• 3 numbers saved in my phone: 988 • 1-800-662-4357 • 911

• 1 thing I will remove or put out of reach tonight

• 1 safe place I can go

Do This Today
Save the three crisis numbers in my phone
Fill in my 3 people (or call SAMHSA if I have none)
Remove or hand off one thing within reach

Prayer

Father, be my refuge tonight. Put safe people in my path and give me the courage to use the phone. Guard my body and my mind. Amen.

Day 3 of 7

Feed the Body

1 Kings 19:6 (ESV)“And behold, there was at his head a cake baked on hot stones and a jar of water. And he ate and drank and lay down again.”

Elijah was burned out and begging to die, and the first thing God did was feed him and let him sleep. Sometimes the holy thing is a sandwich and a nap.

If you have been using heavily, do NOT quit alone or cold if the substance is alcohol, benzodiazepines, or opioids — withdrawal can be dangerous. Call a doctor, an ER, or SAMHSA (1-800-662-4357) first.

Tool: H.A.L.T. Check

• Hungry — when did I last eat?

• Angry — what am I holding?

• Lonely — who have I talked to today?

• Tired — how many hours did I sleep?

Do This Today
Drink a full glass of water, eat something real
Set a bedtime and put the phone across the room
Book or call one medical/treatment contact

Prayer

Lord, You fed Elijah before You gave him an assignment. Feed me. Help me take care of this body You gave me. Amen.

Day 4 of 7

Know Your Triggers

1 Corinthians 10:13 (ESV)“No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation he will provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.”

The corner didn't only live on a street. It lives in a time of day, a text message, a payday, a person, a feeling. Map it and you can walk around it.

Cravings peak and pass, usually within minutes. You don't have to win forever. You have to outlast the next fifteen minutes.

Tool: The 15-Minute Craving Plan

• My top 3 triggers (people / places / feelings / times)

• When a craving hits I will: move, drink water, call ______

• My fifteen-minute rule: I wait, then I decide again

Do This Today
Write my top 3 triggers
Pick my “call first” person
Practice the 15-minute wait once, phone timer on

Prayer

God, You said there's a way out. Open my eyes to it when the craving comes, and let my feet move toward it before my head talks me out of it. Amen.

Day 5 of 7

Break the Isolation

Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (ESV)“Two are better than one... For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow.”

Shame says hide. God says come out. Isolation is where relapse does its best work — and where the enemy tells you nobody wants to hear it.

You need people who know the truth about you. Recovery meetings, a sponsor, a counselor, and a church that doesn't perform — look for people who tell the truth and stay.

Tool: The Reach-Out Script

• “Hey, I need help. I'm not okay and I don't want to do this alone. Can I call you today?”

• Meeting / group I will attend this week (day + time)

• Person I will text before noon tomorrow

Do This Today
Send the reach-out text to one person
Find one meeting or group and put it on my calendar
Sit with one safe person for ten minutes, no phone

Prayer

Lord, break the lie that I'm too much or too far gone. Send me people who will sit in it with me and point me back to You. Amen.

Day 6 of 7

Own It — Without Drowning in It

Proverbs 28:13 (ESV)“Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy.”

Grace is not permission. You can be forgiven and still owe repair. You can also be the one who was harmed and be wrongly carrying blame that belongs to somebody else — if that's you, put it down today.

Owning it is a plan, not a punishment: name it, tell the truth, make it right where it's safe, and change the pattern. Repair never means putting yourself back in danger.

Tool: Own / Repair / Release

• What I did that is mine to own

• Where repair is safe and possible (and one first step)

• What was done TO me that is NOT mine to carry

Do This Today
Write my “Own” list — facts, not self-hatred
Write one first repair step (or “none safe right now”)
Read the “Release” list aloud and hand it to God

Prayer

Lord, show me what is mine and what isn't. Give me courage to repair what I can and grace to release what I can't. Amen.

Day 7 of 7

Plant Something

John 15:5 (ESV)“I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.”

The bando taught you to survive. The vineyard teaches you to grow — and growing is slow, and it includes pruning. You did not become her overnight. You become her every time you choose not to go back.

A relapse is not the end of the story. It's information. Call your people, tell the truth, and start again from the next right step.

Tool: My Next 30 Days

• Daily anchor (10 minutes with God, same time)

• Weekly anchor (meeting / counselor / church)

• One thing I am building (skill, job step, budget, hobby)

• The person I will check in with every Sunday

Do This Today
Choose my daily and weekly anchors
Put them on my calendar right now
Tell someone my plan for the next 30 days

Prayer

Jesus, You are the vine. I'm done running on my own supply. Keep me connected to You, and help me choose the next right thing tomorrow, and the day after that. Amen.
