# Corner to Crown: editor's verification notes

Internal checklist for 7HE H0LY R3UP. Not part of the printed book.

## Verify before selling or distributing
1. **Meqabyan (1–3 Meqabyan).** The book deliberately quotes nothing from it. Day 27 only says the books exist, are not the Maccabees, and tell of believers under pressure from rulers. Pull exact passages from a vetted Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo translation before adding any quotation or chapter reference.
2. **1 Enoch references used:** 6–8 (Watchers), 7:1 and 8:3 (charms, enchantments, cutting of roots and plants), 15 (origin of evil spirits), 1:9 (quoted in Jude 14–15). Check chapter and verse numbers against the translation you publish with, since Ethiopic and Charles numbering differ in places.
3. **Jubilees references used:** 8–10 (allotment of territories), 10:1–13 (Noah's prayer, Mastema's tenth, angels teach plant remedies), 10:29–34 (Canaan settling land not allotted to him). Same check.
4. **ESV permission.** The copyright page carries Crossway's standard notice. Confirm the quotation count and any commercial-use terms with Crossway before selling.
5. **Hotline numbers** (U.S.): 988; 1-800-799-7233 (text START to 88788); 1-888-373-7888 (text 233733); SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357. Re-check before each print run.
6. **Cover art is vector line art, not a photograph.** A realistic cover (turquoise leather, distressed denim, mason jars catching light) still needs to be generated or photographed separately.

## Claims deliberately softened
- "10%" of Mastema is presented as *limited, leashed authority* (Jubilees 10:8–9, Job 1:12), not a statistic.
- "Bloodline blocks" is presented as *patterns that travel through homes*, balanced by Ezekiel 18:20. The book does not teach inherited guilt or a literal curse mechanism.
- Pharmakeia is tied to numbing as *application*, with a standing note to keep prescribed medication and treatment.
- Coloring and worksheets are labeled as practice, not as spiritual transactions.
