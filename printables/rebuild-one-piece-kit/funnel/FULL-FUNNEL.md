## 8. What to measure (no invented targets)

Track these weekly. Don't judge anything until you have a few weeks of data.

| Stage | Number to watch | What to do if it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | Comments with a keyword per post | Change the hook (first line), not the offer |
| DM | Replies to the first DM | Shorten the first DM |
| Opt-in page | Visitors who give an email | Tighten the headline; check the page loads on a phone |
| Email 1 | Open rate and downloads | Try a plainer subject line |
| Email 3 | Clicks to the kit page | Move the kit mention earlier, or soften it |
| Product page | Visitors who add to cart | Check the first image and first line; test the price |
| Checkout | Orders started vs finished | Remove fields, check mobile |
| Refunds | Any refund request | Read every one. It tells you what the page promised that the kit didn't. |
| Replies | People who reply with a story | Reply personally. This is your best feedback. |

**Decision rule:** change one thing at a time for two weeks. Don't change the product, price, and page at once.
