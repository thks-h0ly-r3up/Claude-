## Full description

**D.O.A. is God's favorite starting point.**

I'm Hellshaker. I've lived through addiction, abuse, and grief, and I'm still rebuilding with Big Boy beside me. I made this kit for the version of me who didn't know where to start.

Nobody rebuilds everything in a day. What works is one honest step, then the next. This kit gives your hands something real to do (cut, glue, write, fold, pray) while your heart catches up. It also gives you tools for the hard moments, because a pretty printable that doesn't help at 2 a.m. isn't worth your money.

### What's inside (37 pages, US Letter)

**Make it (the hands-on part)**
- **Rebuild Board:** 4x4 puzzle board plus 16 word pieces and 2 blank "your word" pieces. Easy-cut or puzzle-cut.
- **16 Word Cards:** each with a hard truth, a Scripture, and one action step (Identify, Surrender, Tell the Truth, Release, Replace, Renew, Heal, Forgive, Set Boundaries, Take Action, Trust, Grow, Keep Going, Find Purpose, Encourage Others, Freedom).
- **12 Scripture Bookmarks:** each with a "Today I'm choosing" prompt.
- **Next Right Step Jar:** 60 slips (Body, Mind, Spirit, People, Practical), jar labels, gift tags, and the rules of the jar.

**Walk it (the plan)**
- **7-Day Rebuild Plan:** one page a day with a note from me, Scripture, three honest prompts, a prayer, a craving/stress check, and a daily checklist.
- **30-Day Tracker** and **Weekly Review** so the work doesn't stop on day 7.
- **4 Prayers** (morning, when the craving comes, night, for the ones you love).

**Use it (the real-life tools)**
- **When It Hits:** a six-step, fifteen-minute plan for cravings and hard moments, with a fill-in for your danger times, triggers, and reasons.
- **If You Slipped:** relapse is not resignation. What to do in the first hour, and how to learn the pattern without the shame spiral.
- **Boundary Builder:** access levels (Open, Limited, Distance, Closed), sentences you can steal, and a reminder that forgiveness is not access.
- **Safe People and Support List:** your people, your meetings, and U.S. hotlines in one place.
- **Practical Rebuild Checklist:** IDs, health, home, work, legal, faith and people.
- **Money Reset:** one page, no shame, just numbers and the next call.
- **Certificate of Completion** with a witness line.
