## POST 2: The Craving Plan (image: `ad-c-one-honest-step.png`)

**A craving is a wave. Not the ocean.**

&nbsp;

It's 2 a.m.
Everything in you says *just once.*
And you're sure you're the only one who's ever felt this.

You're not.
I've been there.
It felt like the room was closing in.

&nbsp;

*"There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful... and will with the temptation also make a way to escape."*
(1 Corinthians 10:13)

**Common** means you're not the exception.
**A way to escape** means there's a door. Your job is to walk toward it.

&nbsp;

**The 15-minute plan:**

**1. Set a timer for 15 minutes.**
You're not deciding anything until it rings. Waves rise, peak, and fall.

**2. Run HALT.**
Hungry? Angry? Lonely? Tired? Fix the first one you find.

**3. Call a real person.**
Not a text you'll never send. A call. No answer? Call the next one.

&nbsp;

I put this on one page, with blanks for **your** danger times, **your** triggers, and **your** reasons. Fill it out while you're calm.

**Need more than paper?**
Call or text **988**. Call **SAMHSA at 1-800-662-4357** (free, 24/7). Emergency? **911.**

*Stopping alcohol or benzos suddenly can be dangerous. Talk to a doctor first.*

## POST 3: Boundaries (image: `previews/09-boundary-builder.jpg`)

**Forgiveness is not access.**

&nbsp;

You forgave them.
Then they showed up like nothing happened.
And now you feel guilty for keeping the door shut.

Me too.

Somebody told you *"a real Christian would let them back in."*
**That's not what the Bible says.**

&nbsp;

*"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life."*
(Proverbs 4:23)

**Guard it.** That's a command, not a sin.

God forgives on repentance.
**Trust gets rebuilt on evidence.**
You can release the debt and **keep the gate locked.**

&nbsp;

**The 3-step gate:**

**1. Pick the access level.**
Open. Limited. Distance. Closed.

**2. Write the sentence.**
*"I love you, and I won't be around when you're using."*
*"I forgive you. I'm not available for that anymore."*

**3. Decide what you'll do if it's crossed.**
Not what you'll say. What you'll **do.**

&nbsp;

*If someone is dangerous: don't confront them. Make a safety plan. National DV Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 (text START to 88788).*

I understand why they are the way they are.
**You're still allowed to protect yourself.**
