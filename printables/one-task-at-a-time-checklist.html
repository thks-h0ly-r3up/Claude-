Before You Start — A Prayer Lord, quiet the noise. There's a hundred things pulling at me right now, and I can't carry all of them at once — and You never asked me to. Help me put my hand to the one thing You've called me to right now, and give me peace to set the rest down without guilt or panic. Give me the discipline to write down what tries to steal my focus instead of chasing it. One task. One step. One row of the vineyard at a time. In Jesus' name, amen.
“Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with all your might.” — Ecclesiastes 9:10
