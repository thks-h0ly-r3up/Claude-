# PART I — TRAUMA: WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED TO YOU

## 1.1 The definition they never gave you

Trauma is not the event. Trauma is what the event **left inside you** when your nervous system could not finish the response it needed to finish.

Say it plainer: something happened that was too much, too fast, too soon, or for too long, and you had no way out and nobody to run to. So your body did the only thing it could — it froze the moment and carried it.

You are not still reacting to the past. You are reacting to the **unfinished present** the past left in your body.

## 1.2 The four responses — and why yours wasn't a choice

| Response | What it looks like | What it protected |
|---|---|---|
| **Fight** | Rage, arguing, provoking, control, "I'll ruin it before it ruins me" | Your right to exist |
| **Flight** | Leaving, moving, cutting off, ghosting, staying too busy to feel | Your body's exit |
| **Freeze** | Going blank, dissociating, watching yourself from the ceiling | Your mind, when the body couldn't run |
| **Fawn** | People-pleasing, over-apologizing, becoming useful to whoever is dangerous | Your life, by making you not-worth-hurting |

**Most abuse survivors are fawners who got called "sweet."** Most addiction survivors are flighters who got called "unreliable." Most children of chaos are freezers who got called "spacey."

They were not personality traits. They were survival strategies that kept working after the danger left.

## 1.4 What trauma does to the body (so you stop thinking you're broken)

Your body is not malfunctioning. It is running an old program on a current threat setting.

- **Amygdala** — your smoke alarm. After trauma it's set to go off at toast. Hypervigilance, startle, "I can read a room in three seconds."
- **Hippocampus** — your filing clerk. Trauma disrupts it, so the memory never gets filed as **past**. That's why it feels like now. That's a flashback. It is not weakness and it is not demonic — it is a filing error with a body attached.
- **Prefrontal cortex** — your reasoning. It goes offline under threat. That's why you "know better" and still do it. Knowing lives upstairs; the alarm lives downstairs; downstairs wins.
- **Vagus nerve** — your brake pedal. Trauma wears the brake. That's the shutdown, the numbness, the sudden exhaustion.
- **Cortisol and adrenaline** — the fuel. Years of it is why your stomach, your immune system, your sleep, your cycle, your teeth, your back all went down at once.

**Trauma is stored in the body.** That's why talking alone doesn't finish it. You cannot think your way out of a body-level wound. You have to bring the body.

### Body-based resets (use these all through the workbook)

Learn these NOW, before the hard pages.

**1. The 4-7-8 breath (drops the alarm)**
In through the nose 4 counts. Hold 7. Out through the mouth 8, long and loud. Four rounds. The long exhale is the part that works.

**2. 5-4-3-2-1 (ends a flashback)**
Out loud: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Then say the date and your age. "It is [today's date]. I am [your age]. That was then."

**3. Cold water on the wrists and face (hard reset)**
Drops heart rate fast. Works when nothing else does.

**4. Orienting (tells the body the room is safe)**
Slowly turn your head and look at every corner of the room. Let your eyes land on the exit. Your body needs to SEE there's a door.

**5. Push the wall**
Palms flat on a wall, push hard for 10 seconds, release. Gives the fight response somewhere to go.

**6. Name it to tame it**
"I am having a trauma response. This is not danger. This is memory."

# PART II — THE SPIRITUAL REALM: THE CRASH COURSE

## 2.1 Start here: there are two realms and one is more real

The physical realm is what you can measure. The spiritual realm is what generates it.

> "We look not to the things that are seen but to the things that are unseen. For the things that are seen are transient, but the things that are unseen are eternal." — 2 Corinthians 4:18

> "For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness." — Ephesians 6:12

This is not metaphor language. Paul means **there is an actual opposing structure.** It has ranks. It has strategy. It has a limit.

## 2.2 The three-way error most people make

| Error | What it sounds like | Why it wrecks you |
|---|---|---|
| **Everything is demonic** | "The devil gave me depression." | You spend years binding a spirit that was a thyroid, a trauma loop, or a bad marriage. Nothing improves, so you conclude God failed. |
| **Nothing is spiritual** | "It's all just psychology." | You white-knuckle a war you can't see and lose ground you don't know you're standing on. |
| **It's spiritual so I don't need help** | "I just need more faith." | You die of something treatable while praying. |

**The THKS position: it is almost always BOTH, and you work both.**

A wound can be real trauma AND an open door. A craving can be real neurochemistry AND a familiar spirit exploiting a groove. A rage can be your amygdala AND something riding it. Treat the body. Close the door. Both.

## 2.3 What's actually in the realm

**God** — not a force, a Person. Sovereign, present, the only one with unlimited authority. Not the author of what was done to you.

**The Holy Spirit** — God living *in* you if you're His. Your comfort, your conviction, your power, your discernment. Conviction feels like clean grief and points to a specific thing with a way out. Condemnation feels like drowning and points at your whole self with no exit. **Learn the difference. It will save you years.**

**Jesus** — the one who already won. Colossians 2:15: He disarmed the powers and made a public spectacle of them. This matters because it means you are not fighting for a victory — you are **enforcing** one.

**Angels** — messengers and warriors. Sent. Not summoned by you, not worshiped by you.

**Satan** — a created being. Fallen. Limited. A liar (John 8:44), a thief (John 10:10), an accuser (Revelation 12:10). **He is not omnipresent and he is not the opposite of God.** He does not know your thoughts. He studies your patterns. That's why patterns matter.

**Demonic ranks** — Ephesians 6:12 lists rulers, authorities, cosmic powers, spiritual forces. There's structure. Practical version: some things attack individuals, some attach to bloodlines, some sit over places and systems. Which is why "I moved and it followed me" is a real experience and "my whole family has this" is a real pattern.

**Your own flesh** — Galatians 5. Not a demon. Your untrained appetite. **Most of what people try to cast out, they need to crucify.** You cannot rebuke a habit you keep feeding.

## 2.4 Open doors — how access happens

Access is not random. There is usually a **door**. Not to shame you — to give you something to close.

Check honestly:

- [ ] **Agreement with a lie** — every time you said "I'm worthless," you signed something.
- [ ] **Occult contact** — tarot, mediums, Ouija, spells, santería, crystals for power, "harmless" psychics, astrology as authority, rituals you did as a kid for protection because nobody else was protecting you.
- [ ] **Sexual soul ties** — 1 Corinthians 6:16. Bodies are joining instruments. Trauma included. This is why some people you barely knew still have a hook in you.

## 2.5 Your actual authority — read this twice

You are not begging. You are not negotiating. You are not equal-and-opposite.

- **Luke 10:19** — "I have given you authority to tread on serpents and scorpions... and nothing shall hurt you."
- **Ephesians 2:6** — you are seated *with Him in the heavenly places.* Positional. Above, not below.
- **James 4:7** — submit to God, THEN resist the devil. **That order is not optional.** Resisting without submitting is how people get hurt.
- **1 John 4:4** — greater is He in you than he in the world.
- **Revelation 12:11** — they overcame by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony. **Your story is a weapon.** That's not a slogan. Telling the truth about what happened to you breaks the power of the secret it was hiding in.

**What authority is not:** a volume contest. You don't need to scream. Jesus rebuked with a sentence. Loud is for your own fear, not for their ears.

## 2.6 THE RENOUNCING — the actual words

Do this out loud. Out loud matters — the spirit realm responds to declared agreement, not private opinion. Do it with one trustworthy believer present if you can. If you can't, do it anyway.

**Step 1 — Get under cover.**
> "Father, I come to You through Jesus Christ, who died for me and rose again. I am Yours. I submit myself to You — my body, my mind, my memory, my will, my future. I need You for this, not my own strength. Cover me in the blood of Jesus."

**Step 2 — Confess what's yours.**
> "I confess my part. Not what was done to me — my part. [Name it specifically. No lists of 'anything and everything.' Be specific. Specific is where power is.] I repent. I turn from it. I receive Your forgiveness, and I refuse to keep paying for what You already paid for."

**Step 3 — Forgive, by name.**
> "I choose to forgive [name]. Not because it was okay. It was not okay. Because I refuse to be their jailer and their prisoner at the same time. I release them to You and out of my hands. I take back what I gave them access to. I cancel the debt I have been trying to collect."

*(This will not feel true the first time. Do it anyway. Feeling follows obedience, sometimes by months.)*

**Step 4 — Renounce every door.**
> "In the name of Jesus Christ I renounce [each door from Work Page 7, one at a time, out loud]. I break every agreement I made with it, knowingly or unknowingly. I revoke all permission. I close that door and I seal it with the blood of Jesus."

**Step 5 — Break vows and self-curses.**
> "I break every vow I made in pain. Specifically: [say each one]. I renounce it. I release myself from it in Jesus' name. It has no more claim on my life."

**Step 6 — Cut soul ties.**
> "In Jesus' name I sever every ungodly soul tie between me and [name, or 'the one whose name I don't know']. I return to them what is theirs. I call back to myself what is mine — my body, my mind, my peace, my identity, my future. What God did not join, I do not keep."

**Step 7 — Command it to go.**
> "Every spirit that came in through those doors — I am not your house anymore. I am bought, sealed, and occupied. In the name of Jesus Christ, leave. Go where He sends you. You may not transfer to my children, my family, or my home. Go now."

**Step 8 — Fill it.**
Do not skip this. Luke 11:24–26 — an empty house gets reoccupied, worse. Deliverance without discipleship is a revolving door.
> "Holy Spirit, fill every place that just emptied. Fill it with Your presence, Your peace, Your truth, Your love. Where fear lived, put love. Where shame lived, put sonship. Where craving lived, put satisfaction. Occupy every room."

**Step 9 — Declare.**
> "I belong to God. I am forgiven, I am free, and I am not going back. What tried to end me is going to be the testimony that frees somebody else. In Jesus' name. Amen."

# PART III — THE CHOSEN ONE: THE DEEP DIVE

## 3.1 First, the correction

The internet sold "chosen one" as special. Scripture sells it as **sent.**

Chosen is not a rank. It is an **assignment.** It does not mean God likes you more. It means God has something for you to carry that most people will not carry, and He is going to prepare you for it in ways that will look, from the outside, like He abandoned you.

> "You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit." — John 15:16

Appointed **that you should go.** Chosen is a verb with a destination.

## 3.2 The pattern — every single one of them

Look at the pattern and find yourself in it:

| Who | The early wound | The wilderness | The assignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joseph** | Betrayed and trafficked by his own blood | 13 years — slavery, false accusation, prison, forgotten | Saved the nations, and the family that sold him |
| **Moses** | Born under a death order, raised in the enemy's house, identity split | 40 years on the back side of the desert after a killing | Led a whole people out |
| **David** | Overlooked youngest, left with sheep | Years hunted by the king who was supposed to bless him | King, and the voice of everyone who ever cried out |
| **Hannah** | Barren, mocked, misunderstood — the priest thought she was drunk while she prayed | Years of unanswered asking | Gave the nation its prophet |
| **Esther** | Orphan, taken into a king's harem — she was not "picked," she was collected | Hidden, silent, one wrong move from death | Stopped a genocide |
| **Paul** | Was the abuser first. Blood on his hands. | Blinded, then years unknown in Arabia and Tarsus | Wrote most of the New Testament |
| **Jesus** | Rumored illegitimate, refugee child, hometown rejection | 40 days of hunger and direct temptation | Everything |

**The pattern:**
1. Marked early — often by wound, not by celebration.
2. Isolated — removed from the people who should have carried you.
3. Delayed — longer than you can stand.
4. Misunderstood — called crazy, drunk, dramatic, too much, divisive.
5. Broken in a specific spot — the exact place your assignment will require humility.
6. Then used — and the fruit is always in the shape of the wound.

**Read that last one again. The fruit is always in the shape of the wound.**

Joseph's assignment was famine and family. He was starved of family. Moses' assignment was deliverance. He was the one who got delivered out of the water. Paul's assignment was grace. He was the one who least deserved it.

**Your assignment is in the shape of your wound.** That's not poetry. That's the pattern. That is why the enemy went after that exact spot early — he read the blueprint before you did.

## 3.3 The cost of being chosen — the unfiltered list

Nobody prints this part. Here it is.

**1. Isolation that isn't punishment.** You will lose people. Some you'll grieve for years. Not all of it is attack — some of it is God clearing a table so He can seat the right people at it. But it will feel identical to abandonment. Both can be true.

**2. Being misunderstood by good people.** Not just enemies. Pastors. Family. Friends who love you. They will call your calling pride, your grief drama, your boundaries bitterness, your urgency instability.

**3. Delay that looks like denial.** Joseph, thirteen years. Moses, forty. You will get a promise and then watch the opposite happen for a long time. **The promise doesn't fail in the delay. It gets forged there.**

**4. The specific attack.** The enemy fights hardest where he's losing most. If the attack on your life has been disproportionate — if it's been *weirdly* much — read that as intelligence about what you're carrying, not evidence that you're cursed.

**5. Repeat temptation at the same fault line.** Whatever nearly took you out will come back with a suit on. Every time you step up in assignment, it will knock. Not because you failed. Because it's the known door.

**6. Being made to carry it without credit.** Sometimes you will do the thing and someone else will get thanked. If you need the credit, you cannot carry the weight.

**7. The loneliness of the middle.** Too healed for your old crowd. Too raw for the polished ones. That gap is where most called people quit. **You are not supposed to stay in the gap. You are supposed to build in it.** That's what THKS is.

**8. Grief that comes back on a schedule.** Anniversaries. Songs. Smells. Being chosen does not exempt you from missing the ones you lost. Brandi's absence does not get smaller because the work is real. It gets *carried better.* Different thing.

## 3.5 The transition: from victim to survivor to steward

# PART IV — SURVIVOR OF ADDICTION AND ABUSE

## 4.1 The truth about how the two braid together

Addiction and abuse are not two separate problems that happened to the same person. They feed each other.

**Abuse creates the hole. Addiction fills it. Addiction then puts you in rooms where more abuse happens. More abuse deepens the hole.** Around and around.

This is why "just stop using" fails. You are not asking someone to drop a habit. You are asking them to sit in an unmedicated wound with no skills and no safety. Of course they went back. Anyone would.

**Which means: you cannot get sober and heal the trauma later.** You have to run them together. Sobriety without trauma work is a countdown. Trauma work while actively using is a spin cycle. Both, at once, slowly.

## 4.2 What addiction actually is

Three layers. Work all three or it comes back.

**Layer 1 — Physical.** Real dependence. Real receptors. Real withdrawal. Requires medical help sometimes. Not a moral issue.

**Layer 2 — Psychological.** The relief. Addiction is not a love of the substance. It is a love of the *gap in the pain.* It worked. That's the problem — it worked, right up until it started charging interest.

**Layer 3 — Spiritual.** Every addiction is a false savior. It promises what only God gives: peace, escape, belonging, numbness from shame, courage, rest. It's a counterfeit communion.

> "My people have committed two evils: they have forsaken me, the fountain of living waters, and dug for themselves cisterns — broken cisterns that can hold no water." — Jeremiah 2:13

The cistern isn't the sin. **Digging your own** is.

## STEP 7 — CRAVING PROTOCOL (memorize this)

7. **Pray specific and short.** "Jesus, I can't do this minute. You hold it." Not eloquent. Effective.
8. **Play the tape forward.** Not the first hour. The 48th. The phone call you'd have to make. The face of the person you'd have to tell. Write it now so it's ready:

# PART VI — ROADMAP: BREAKING GENERATIONAL CURSES

## 6.1 What it is and what it isn't

**It is:** a pattern of sin, wound, agreement, and spiritual access that runs down a bloodline until somebody stands in the gap and stops it.

- "Visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children to the third and fourth generation" — Exodus 20:5
- "Our fathers sinned and are no more, and we bear their iniquities." — Lamentations 5:7

**It isn't:** God punishing you for what your grandfather did. Read Ezekiel 18 and Jeremiah 31:29–30 — God explicitly says the son does **not** bear the father's guilt. And Galatians 3:13 — "Christ redeemed us from the curse."

**So how is it both?** Because **guilt** doesn't transfer, but **consequence, pattern, wound, and access** absolutely do. You didn't inherit their sin. You inherited the *terrain.* The debt was paid; the door was left standing open, and nobody in four generations walked over and shut it.

**That's your job. That's the whole assignment. You're the one who closes it.**

Practical layers, all real at once:
- **Behavioral** — you learned it. Modeled, absorbed, normal to you.
- **Biological** — epigenetics. Stress response literally passes down. Trauma changes gene expression in offspring. This is measurable.
- **Systemic** — poverty, incarceration, no education, no assets. Real walls.
- **Spiritual** — access granted by covenant, vow, occult practice, or unrepented pattern, never revoked.

You break all four with four different tools. Most people try one and wonder why it didn't hold.

# PART VII — LETTING PEOPLE GO, AND SITTING SILENT WITH GOD

## 7.1 Why letting go is the hardest page in the book

Because you're not letting go of a person. You're letting go of:
- the version of them you needed them to become
- the apology you're still owed
- the hope that this time it would be different
- the role you've played your whole life
- and the belief that if you hold on hard enough, love will finally show up

**You are grieving something that never existed.** That's why it hurts worse than a funeral. There's no body and nobody sends flowers.

## 7.2 The four kinds of letting go

| Type | What it means | The hard truth |
|---|---|---|
| **Release to God** | They stay in your life, you stop carrying their outcome | You can love someone and not be their savior |
| **Boundary** | They stay, with limits | Boundaries are not punishments; they're property lines |
| **Distance** | Less access, not zero | You can go low-contact without a declaration |
| **Cut off** | No contact | Sometimes required. Sometimes the only way anyone survives |

**You are not obligated to give unlimited access to someone who is actively harming you, and no Scripture requires it.** Jesus withdrew constantly (Luke 5:16, John 6:15). Paul named people and said avoid them (2 Timothy 4:14–15, Romans 16:17). "Turn the other cheek" was about personal insult, not standing in a house fire.

## 7.3 The honest diagnostic — is this letting go, or is it quitting on someone?

## 7.4 The specific one: letting go of someone still using

This is its own hell, and if you've lost someone to it, read this slowly.

**What you can do:** love them, tell the truth, keep the door of relationship open while closing the door of enabling, keep Narcan in your house, know the number to call, pray without ceasing, and be someone they can come back to.

**What you cannot do:** want it more than they do and have that work. Save someone who isn't reaching. Control the outcome. Be the reason. Trade your life for theirs.

**The hardest sentence in this workbook:** You can do everything right and still lose them.

And here's the other side of it: **if you lost someone, that does not mean you did it wrong.** It means addiction is lethal and they were sick and you were not God. Read that again. You were not, and are not, God. That was never your job and it was never in your hands.

## 7.5 How to actually do it — the release

Don't do this halfway. Halfway is how you end up doing it fourteen times.

**Step 1 — Say the truth about what it was.** Out loud. Not the sanitized version. Not the villain version either. The true one.

**Step 2 — Grieve it like a death.** Because it is one. Let yourself cry for a person who is still alive. This is legitimate grief and it needs the same space.

**Step 3 — Forgive without reconciling.** Forgiveness is between you and God. Reconciliation requires two people and repentance. **You can be fully forgiving and fully unavailable.** Nobody taught us that and it changes everything.

**Step 4 — Return what's theirs.**
> "I give back the shame that was never mine. I give back the blame. I give back the story they wrote about me. I give back the responsibility for their choices. I take back my name, my voice, my body, my future, and my peace."

**Step 5 — Cut the tie.** (2.6, Step 6.)

**Step 6 — Set the actual boundary in the actual world.** Block the number. Change the lock. Tell the family member who relays messages to stop. Boundaries that exist only in your heart are wishes.

**Step 7 — Plan for the pullback.** They will reach out. Usually right when you get peaceful. Decide now what you'll do, because deciding in the moment is how you get pulled back in.

## 7.6 SITTING SILENT WITH GOD

This is the last section because it's the one that holds everything else up. And it's the one you'll resist most, because silence is where the pain lives and you've spent your whole life outrunning it.

### Why silence is the hardest spiritual discipline for survivors

Noise was safety. If it was quiet, you could hear them coming. If it was quiet, you had to feel it. So you filled every second — music, TV, scrolling, people, work, using. You are not undisciplined. **You are guarded.**

Silence will feel like danger before it feels like peace. That's not failure. That's a nervous system that learned quiet meant something bad was about to happen.

### What the Scripture actually says

- **1 Kings 19:11–12** — Elijah, suicidal and burned out, looks for God in wind, earthquake, fire. God's in "a sound of thin silence." The whisper comes *after* God feeds him and lets him sleep twice. **Note the order: food, sleep, then the whisper.** God did not rebuke his exhaustion. He fed it.
- **Psalm 46:10** — "Be still and know." The Hebrew *rapha* is closer to "let go, drop your hands, stop striving."
- **Psalm 62:1** — "For God alone my soul waits in silence."
- **Lamentations 3:26** — "It is good that one should wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD."
- **Isaiah 30:15** — "In returning and rest you shall be saved; in quietness and in trust shall be your strength." And then, brutally honest: "But you were unwilling."

### THE PRACTICE — start absurdly small

**Week 1 — 2 minutes a day.**
Yes, two. Sit. Feet on the floor. Eyes open if closed feels unsafe — that's allowed and it's smart. Say once: "Jesus, I'm here. You don't have to say anything. I'm not going anywhere for two minutes."

Then nothing. No agenda. No asking. No performance.

When your mind runs — and it will — don't fight it. Say "later" and come back. You'll come back forty times in two minutes. **Coming back IS the practice.** The returning is the prayer.

**Week 2 — 5 minutes.**
**Week 3 — 10 minutes.**
**Week 4 — 15 minutes.** Stay here. 15 minutes daily beats an hour on Sunday.

### What to do when silence gets loud

| What comes up | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Panic / tight chest** | Open your eyes. Orient (look at all four corners). Breathe 4-7-8. Come back tomorrow, shorter. You are not failing. |
| **Memories flooding** | Keep a notebook beside you. Write the memory down, one line. Tell it "you're on the list." Return to the silence. |
| **Rage** | Let it be there. Rage in God's presence is biblical. Read a psalm of imprecation out loud if you need words. |
| **Accusation** ("God's disgusted with you") | That voice isn't His. Answer it once — "Romans 8:1" — and return. Don't argue with it. |
| **Nothing at all, for weeks** | Normal. Faithfulness in the dry season is worth more than ecstasy in the easy one. Keep showing up. |
| **You fall asleep** | Then you needed sleep. God is not offended. Elijah slept twice before the whisper. |

### The four postures of silence

Rotate these. Don't do just one.

1. **Silent presence** — nothing. Just there. "I'm here."
2. **Silent listening** — one question, then quiet. "What do You want me to know today?" Then wait. Write down what comes; test it later against Scripture and wise people. If it accuses, shames, or condemns you, it isn't Him.
3. **Silent surrender** — hands open, palms up. Physically hand over one thing. Name it. Leave it. Go.
4. **Silent lament** — sit with the grief without fixing it. Don't explain it to God. Don't excuse Him. Just bring it. Psalm 88 ends in darkness and God still printed it.

# CLOSING: THE COMMISSION

You have now done work most people never do. Not because they're weaker — because nobody handed them the pages.

Here's what's true now:

**What happened to you was real.** Nobody gets to minimize it, including you.

**What you did while surviving is forgiven.** Not excused — paid for. Stop billing yourself.

**The spiritual realm is real, you have authority in it, and you are not a victim in it.** Seated, not cowering.

**Being chosen cost you what it costs everybody.** You are not being punished. You are being prepared, and the shape of your wound is the shape of your assignment.

**The addiction is breakable.** Not by willpower — by architecture, honesty, community, medicine where needed, and the Spirit of God. Step by step, day by day, one craving at a time.

**The line stops with you.** Four generations of it and you're the one standing in the gap. That's not a burden. That's the whole reason you're still breathing.

**You are allowed to let people go.** Even blood. Even the ones you love. Loving someone does not require you to die with them.

**And you are allowed to sit down and be quiet with God**, and bring Him nothing, and produce nothing, and still be fully His.
