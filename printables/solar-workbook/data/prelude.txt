=== 1
title: Start Where You Are
ref: Isaiah 30:15
q: “In returning and rest you shall be saved; in quietness and in trust shall be your strength.” (Isa. 30:15, ESV)
teach: Today is a Saturday, the Sabbath, and it is also the day you decided to begin. I will not call that fate. I will call it a gift of timing. Isaiah says strength comes in returning and rest, in quietness and trust. The people in that passage were unwilling to rest, which is a warning to anyone who learned that stopping is dangerous. You do not have to be ready. You do not have to be sober a certain length, or healed, or sure. You only have to show up, and the first act of showing up is to rest a few minutes and say, “I am here.” Welcome. The lights get set on Wednesday.
intent: God’s pattern is rest first, then work. The first full day people ever had was the Sabbath.
quantum: Analogy: An atom has to take in energy before it can give off light. Light goes out after energy comes in. Resting is how you take in.
canon: Jubilees 2:17–33 says the Sabbath was kept in heaven before it was given to people.
r1: What brought me to this book today?
r2: What am I afraid will happen if I start?
r3: What would resting for ten minutes feel like in my body?
action: Write your starting sentence, then rest for ten minutes. You do not have to feel ready.
s1: Write: “I am starting on ____ (date) and I am here because ____.”
s2: Rest for ten minutes, lying down or sitting, with no phone.
s3: Say out loud: “I am here. That is enough.”
prayer: God, I am here, and I do not feel ready. I am not going to pretend I am. Meet me in this quiet. Give me rest before I do anything else, and let this be a real beginning. In Jesus’ name, amen.
decl: I do not have to be ready to begin. I only have to show up.
cal: Start and rest

=== 2
title: Light for the Road
ref: Psalm 27:1
q: “The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?” (Ps. 27:1, ESV)
teach: David wrote this with enemies close, and he still put light first. Fear is not a sin, and it is not proof of weak faith. It is information. If you are starting something new, fear will talk. The question is whether it gets the only vote. Today, Sunday, the day of light, we do something practical so that fear has less room: we make a support list before we need it. Safe people, safe numbers, safe places. If you have none yet, the numbers on this page count. A list on paper is a lamp you can find in the dark.
intent: God is called light and stronghold in the same breath. He brings both guidance and protection.
quantum: Physics note: A small light is easiest to see where it is darkest. You do not need a big light to find the way.
canon: 1 Enoch 38:4 says the light of the Lord of Spirits will be seen on the face of the holy.
r1: What am I most afraid of as I start?
r2: Who or what has helped me feel safe before?
r3: What would I want in my pocket on a bad night?
action: Make a one-page support list: three people or places, and three numbers. Keep it where you can find it.
s1: Write three people or places you could turn to, even if one is a church office or a group.
s2: Add the numbers: 988 (call or text), SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357, and 211 for local help.
s3: Put the page where you will find it on a hard night.
prayer: Lord, You are my light. I am afraid of some things and I will tell You which ones. Give me safe people and safe places. Let this list be a lamp I can find when it is dark. Amen.
decl: Fear can talk, and it does not get the only vote.
cal: Support list

=== 3
title: Count the Cost, Make Room
ref: Luke 14:28
q: “For which of you, desiring to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost, whether he has enough to complete it?” (Luke 14:28, ESV)
teach: Jesus is not trying to scare anyone off when He talks about counting the cost. He is being honest, the way a builder is. Rebuilding a life takes time, space, and people, and ten minutes of planning now saves a lot of quitting later. Monday is for boundaries, so today you make room. Pick a daily window, even ten or twenty minutes. Pick a spot. Decide what stays out of that window: the phone, the loudest person, the old habit. Tell one person what you are doing. Being honest about the cost is not unbelief. It is wisdom.
intent: God honors planning. Boundaries around time and place are part of how people finish what they start.
quantum: Analogy: An electron cannot move up a level with a little energy. It needs a set amount to make the jump. Making room is gathering enough for the next step.
canon: Sirach 18:19–20 says to learn before you speak and to care for your health before you fall ill, which is advice about preparing ahead.
r1: What will this cost me in time, comfort, or habit?
r2: What is likely to get in the way of my daily window?
r3: What boundary would protect it?
action: Choose your daily window, your spot, and one thing you will keep out of it. Tell one person.
s1: Pick a time you can keep on a rough day, even ten minutes.
s2: Pick a spot, and decide what stays out of that time.
s3: Tell one safe person what you are starting.
prayer: Father, I want to finish this, so help me count the cost honestly. Show me where I can make room. Give me a boundary I can keep, and let me not be ashamed to ask for help. Amen.
decl: Counting the cost is wisdom, not doubt.
cal: Make room

=== 4
title: Get the Ground Ready
ref: Proverbs 24:27
q: “Prepare your work outside; get everything ready for yourself in the field, and after that build your house.” (Prov. 24:27, ESV)
teach: Tomorrow is Day 1, Wednesday, the fourth day of the creation week, the day God set the lights. Tonight is for the ground. The Proverb says to prepare the field first and build the house after, which is the order a vineyard keeper would follow. Fix the small things: a pen that works, a spot to sit, a Bible or an app, water, a phone alarm. Choose the time you will start tomorrow. If you want to know how the 364-day calendar works, the next pages explain it. For now, get ready. Tuesday is for the vineyard: plant, prune, prepare.
intent: God teaches preparation before building. Good ground comes before good fruit.
quantum: Physics note: A solar panel has to be built and pointed at the sun before it can use the light. Preparation comes before power.
canon: Jubilees 7:1–2 says Noah planted a vineyard and waited until the fourth year for the fruit.
r1: What do I need on hand so tomorrow is easy?
r2: What could make me skip Day 1, and how can I get ahead of it?
r3: What do I want to remember about why I am starting?
action: Set up for Day 1: gather your pen, this book, a Bible or app, and water. Set a phone alarm for your start time.
s1: Gather your pen, this book, and a Bible or app, and put them where you will sit.
s2: Set an alarm for your start time tomorrow.
s3: Write one sentence about why you are starting, and put it on the first page.
prayer: Lord, You prepared the ground before You set the lights. Help me prepare mine. Keep me from skipping what is small, and meet me tomorrow at the start. Amen.
decl: Good ground comes before good fruit.
cal: Set up for Day 1
